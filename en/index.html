About

About VX SPORTS ACADEMY

VX SPORTS ACADEMY is a sports academy led by Ippei Shinozuka that develops football skill and athletic ability for every age, from kids to aspiring athletes.

Former pros and specialist coaches tailor athletic and football training to each player.

  • VX PERSONAL | Personal football coaching (open)
  • VX GYM | Gym for kids to athletes, any age (Nishi-Azabu, Tokyo / opening Jan 2027)
  • VX SOCCER | New U-12・U-9 football team (recruiting)
  • VX APPAREL | Everyday apparel (pre-orders open)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is VX SPORTS ACADEMY?

A sports academy that develops football skill and athletic ability for every age, from kids to aspiring athletes. We run four services: VX PERSONAL (personal football coaching), VX GYM (gym for kids to athletes, any age), VX SOCCER (football team) and VX APPAREL (apparel brand).

Who is the founder?

Founder Ippey Shinozuka is a former professional footballer (midfielder). After Russia U-18 and Spartak Moscow, he played for Yokohama F. Marinos, Omiya Ardija, Kashiwa Reysol and Albirex Niigata, with 95 appearances, 11 goals and 9 assists in the J.League (J1 and J2 league matches).

Where are you located?

VX GYM opens in Nishi-Azabu, Tokyo in January 2027. VX PERSONAL is available in Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and Saitama.

What can I sign up for now?

VX PERSONAL is open (trial session ¥5,500 incl. tax). VX GYM is taking early registrations, VX SOCCER is recruiting U-12 and U-9 players, and VX APPAREL is taking pre-orders.

How can I propose a sponsorship or partnership?

Message us on our official LINE account or by Instagram DM (@vx_sports_academy).

How do I contact you?

Message us on our official LINE account. News is also posted on Instagram (@vx_sports_academy).
