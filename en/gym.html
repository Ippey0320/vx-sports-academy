FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When and where does VX GYM open?

In Nishi-Azabu, Tokyo, planned for January 2027. Early registration is open on LINE.

Who is it for?

Anyone, at any age, from kids to players aiming to become athletes, whatever the sport: football, baseball, basketball, track and more. Classes are grouped by age, up to 8 per coach.

What do you measure?

Power output (W), jump (cm) and speed (s), every session, with progress since joining recorded on a graph.

How much does it cost?

Monthly, incl. tax: once a week (4/month) ¥17,800; twice a week (8/month) ¥28,000. Add "Elite" (a monthly one-on-one plus feedback on results) for +¥15,000.

Does early registration cost anything?

No. It simply puts you first in line for news on the opening date, prices and trial sessions.
