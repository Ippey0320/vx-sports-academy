FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does VX PERSONAL teach?

One-on-one coaching in athleticism (speed, agility, power, core) and football skill, from first touch and passing basics to advanced skills.

How much does it cost?

Trial ¥5,500; regular lesson ¥16,500 each (minimum 4); first month (weekly) ¥66,000; from month two, 3-month plan ¥198,000. All prices include tax.

Is there an age limit?

No. Anyone can join, at any age, from kids to players aiming to become athletes.

Can beginners join?

Yes. We coach beginners through advanced players, matched to each player's level and goals.

Which areas do you cover?

Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and Saitama. Tell us your preferred area on LINE.

How often should I train?

We recommend once a week, consistently. Steady training leads to real progress.

How do I sign up?
